Job Search Gmail Automation

A private job-search workspace for organizing opportunities, preparing resumes and reviewing outreach drafts.

The application combines job listings with an uploaded resume, optional experience library and saved preferences. AI-assisted scoring, resume tailoring and application-answer drafting use those supplied materials. The current deployment is operated for its owner; it is not open for customer registration.

Google connections

Google sign-in protects access to the dashboard. A separate Gmail authorization lets the application create drafts with selected recipients and resume attachments, check draft status, and track replies in existing outreach threads.

The dashboard's Gmail workflow creates drafts for owner review. Google describes the compose permission as allowing both draft management and sending; the application's active workflow remains draft-only.

An optional Google Drive connection backs up prepared resume PDFs in app-managed folders. This connection uses the file-specific Drive permission, not unrestricted access to the entire Drive.

Owner review

The owner reviews application materials and decides whether to send a message or submit an application. AI output and match percentages are assistance, not a hiring guarantee. This deployment does not provide a multi-customer service.
