Use and limitations

Current owner-operated deployment. Updated October 19, 2026.

This application is a personal job-search tool operated for its owner, not a generally available recruitment, placement or multi-customer service. These notes describe its current use; they are not a customer subscription agreement.

Review before acting

The owner is responsible for reviewing generated resumes, application answers, contact details and email drafts. AI output and match scores can be incomplete or incorrect. They do not guarantee an interview, employment, portal acceptance or continuous service availability.

Use only information you are entitled to provide. The job description is a targeting input, not proof of experience. Do not treat generated wording as verified personal history.

Google access and external services

Connecting Google requires the account owner's consent. The active Gmail workflow creates drafts for review; sending a message or submitting an application remains a separate owner decision. Google and other connected services apply their own permissions, quotas, charges and availability conditions. No fixed one- or two-year Google token lifetime is guaranteed.

Read the privacy notice for data processing and removal limitations. Revoking Google access is separate from deleting records already stored by the application or connected services.
