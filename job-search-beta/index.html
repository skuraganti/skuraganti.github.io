Job Search Beta information

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Sashank Kuraganti operates this free, invitation-only beta for invited colleagues and friends. Support and deletion requests: sashankkuraganti@gmail.com.

This is a public information document for the real beta product and a plain-language privacy notice for this beta. It has not been legally reviewed or certified, and it does not promise perfect security or availability.

What invited users can do

Each user signs in with their own Google identity and supplies their own profile, job-search preferences and master resume. An Evidence Library is optional and can be replaced or removed. The beta keeps records, documents, Google grants, queues and prepared work owner-scoped; a missing owner context fails closed.

The product supports job discovery and preparation for the user's own workspace. Results and documents are aids for review, not guarantees. Replaced or removed evidence cannot publish new results from its old source revision.

Current limits

Customer AI-assisted workflows and automated discovery or background preparation are currently not active pending model-route verification. Provider permission, privacy controls and deployment must be verified before those workflows are enabled. No zero-retention, no-training or unlimited-AI promise is made.

The beta does not perform contact research, access LinkedIn, submit job applications or require a marketing mailbox. Gmail is draft-only even though the Google gmail.compose permission technically permits sending. Optional Drive exports use the narrower drive.file permission.

Google data use

Google user data is used only for user-selected sign-in, Gmail draft creation and prepared-file export, including reading matching draft contents or export metadata to check uncertain outcomes. It never sends mail. The beta handles this data under Google's API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. This document does not claim Google certification, verification or approval. Stored Google grants are encrypted and bound to the owning user, provider and connection generation.

Disconnecting a grant stops future beta use of that local grant. It does not by itself remove third-party access in Google Account settings, and drafts or Drive files already created in the user's account remain there.

Deletion and backups

Account deletion removes the live beta account, associated workspace records and documents, and connection data. Limited protected recovery metadata may remain to prevent deleted accounts, documents or disconnected grants from being restored. Prior backups remain under configured retention and may not be erased immediately. Any restore remains quarantined until deletion and revocation safeguards are reviewed. Deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.
