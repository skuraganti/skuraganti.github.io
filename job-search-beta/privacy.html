Privacy information

Reviewed for the invitation-only beta on November 5, 2026. Sashank Kuraganti operates this free beta for invited colleagues and friends. Questions or deletion requests: sashankkuraganti@gmail.com.

This is a plain-language privacy notice for this beta. It has not been legally reviewed or certified, and it does not promise perfect security, uninterrupted availability, zero retention, or no model training.

What the beta handles

To run your own workspace, the beta uses your Google sign-in identity (including name and email), profile and search preferences, the resume and optional Evidence Library you upload, and private per-user job records, prepared documents, queues and work history. Records, documents, grants and work are owner-scoped; missing owner context fails closed. These are your materials, not another applicant's profile or mailbox.

The Evidence Library is optional and replaceable. New results are tied to the current source revision, so an old evidence revision cannot publish new results after it is replaced or removed.

Google access and Limited Use

Sign-in requests only OpenID Connect identity, email and profile access. If you choose to connect Gmail for a draft, the separate gmail.compose permission also technically permits sending mail. The beta creates drafts and reads matching draft contents, including attachments, to check uncertain creation outcomes. It never sends mail. If you choose Drive, the separate drive.file grant supports optional exports of prepared files and checks export metadata to reconcile uncertain outcomes. The beta does not request broad mailbox-read or whole-Drive access.

Stored Google authorization grants are encrypted and bound to your account, the connected feature and its current connection generation.

Google user data is handled only for the sign-in, draft and export features described here. The beta follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This page does not claim Google certification, verification or approval. Drafts and Drive files already created in your Google account remain there if you disconnect or delete your beta account.

The beta does not access your contacts or LinkedIn account, perform contact research, submit job applications, or require a marketing mailbox.

AI processing and automation

Customer AI-assisted workflows and automated discovery or background preparation are currently not active while the model route, provider permission, privacy controls and deployment are being verified. Do not assume that an AI feature, automatic discovery run or background preparation is available or that a particular provider's retention or training practices apply. Any user-initiated workflow still needs the applicable route and safeguards verified before customer use.

Control and deletion

You can edit your profile and preferences, replace your documents, disconnect a Google connection, or request deletion at the contact above. The in-app Delete account action removes the live beta account, associated workspace records and documents, and connection data. Limited protected recovery metadata may remain to prevent deleted accounts, documents or disconnected grants from being restored. It does not delete drafts or files already in your Google account. Prior backups remain under configured retention, and any restore remains quarantined until deletion and revocation safeguards are reviewed. Deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.

Disconnecting in the beta invalidates the local grant for future beta actions. To remove the beta's third-party access from your Google account as well, review and remove it in your Google Account connections. These are distinct actions.
