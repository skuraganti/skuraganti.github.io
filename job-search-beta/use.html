How this beta works

Reviewed for the invitation-only beta on November 5, 2026. Sashank Kuraganti operates this free beta for invited colleagues and friends. Questions: sashankkuraganti@gmail.com.

This is plain-language information about how the beta works. It has not been legally reviewed or certified. The product is real, but this is a limited evaluation with unfinished integrations and no promise of perfect security or availability.

Your workspace

Sign in using your own Google account. You supply your own profile, preferences and master resume; an Evidence Library is optional and replaceable. The beta keeps each user's records, grants, prepared work and history owner-scoped. New results use the current source revision, not replaced or removed evidence from an older revision.

Job matches and tailored documents are aids for your review, not a guarantee of a role, interview, accuracy or an available posting. Check every document and job before using it.

Current availability and actions

Customer AI-assisted workflows and automated discovery or background preparation are currently not active while the model route, provider permission, privacy controls and deployment are being verified. Features that depend on them may be unavailable. No zero-retention, no-training or unlimited-AI promise is made.

Any workflow that becomes available is limited to your own workspace. Gmail is draft-only: you decide whether to create and review each draft, and the beta never automatically sends mail. The beta does not perform contact research, access LinkedIn, submit job applications or require a marketing mailbox.

Google connections

Gmail connection is separate from sign-in and requests gmail.compose, which technically permits creating drafts and sending mail. The beta creates drafts and reads matching draft contents, including attachments, to check uncertain outcomes. It never sends mail. Drive connection separately requests drive.file for optional prepared-file exports and reads export metadata to check uncertain outcomes. Google user data is used only for these user- selected features and handled under Google's API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. This page does not claim Google certification, verification or approval.

Disconnecting a Google connection stops beta use of that local grant, but is not the same as removing third-party access in your Google Account connections. Drafts and Drive files already created in your account remain there.

Leaving the beta

Delete account removes live beta workspace records, documents and connection data. Limited protected recovery metadata may remain to prevent deleted accounts, documents or disconnected grants from being restored. Previously created Google drafts and Drive files remain in your account. Prior backups remain under configured retention and are not erased instantly by the in-app action. Any restore remains quarantined until deletion and revocation safeguards are reviewed. Contact the operator for data questions or a deletion request.

This beta is free and invitation-only. Please review your own output before any external action. See Privacy information for the fuller data description.
